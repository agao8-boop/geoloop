What we model

Every physical layer,
from soil to cost.

GeoSite Advisor runs a seven-stage pipeline. No rule of thumb, no lookup table. Each stage is traceable to a published source.

01
s1_site
01

Subsurface Ground Truth

Soil thermal properties from USGS + SMU geothermal database. GBM model maps rock type to conductivity.

k (W/m·K)  ·  α (m²/s)  ·  Tg (°C)

02
s2_building
02

Building Conditions

16 DOE prototype building types mapped to 17 ASHRAE climate zones via ZIP lookup.

DOE prototypes  ·  ASHRAE 90.1  ·  Envelope class

03
s2_simulation
03

Energy Simulation

8,760-hour ground load profile Q(h) from EnergyPlus with DOE prototype IDFs in Ideal Air Loads mode. Peak heating and cooling extracted per ASHRAE three-pulse conventions.

Q(h) = Qhvac(h)  ·  EnergyPlus IDA  ·  ΔT = Q/UA  ·  8,760 h/yr

04
s4_sizing
04

Borehole Sizing

Philippe et al. (2010) three-pulse method computes total borefield length L, borehole depth H, and count NB. Iterative Tp correction for multi-borehole thermal interaction.

L = (qh·Rb + qm·Rs + qy·Ry) / ΔTg  ·  H = L/NB

05
s6_strategy
05

Hybrid Strategy

LDC cutoff trims peak loads to a supplemental peaker, reducing borefield cost 20–40%.

LDC cutoff  ·  thermal balance  ·  20–40% capex saved

06
s5_cost
06

Capital Cost

Regional drilling rates times total borefield length. Three scenarios with full line-item breakdown.

$/ft × Ltotal  ·  best / base / worst

How it works

A clear path from
building to capital cost.

01

Enter building info

ZIP code, building type, floor area, and envelope class. No spreadsheet, no contractor, no site visit required.

02

Automatic subsurface pull

Soil conductivity and ground temperature from national databases. What costs $5,000 to measure, approximated in seconds.

03

Borefield sizing

EnergyPlus simulates 8,760 hours. ASHRAE three-pulse method sizes the borefield from first principles, traceable to Fourier's law.

04

Capital cost report

Best, base, and worst drilling scenarios benchmarked against conventional HVAC. Hybrid peaker savings quantified as a capital allocation decision.
