dominant load: —
Auto (recommended) — picks the option below that gives the best cost reduction for your building's load profile.

Dominant load shaving (e.g. cooling chiller when cooling dominates) — reduces borefield length by 30–65%, directly cutting drilling cost. Best when upfront cost is the priority.

Non-dominant load shaving (e.g. boiler when cooling dominates) — corrects seasonal thermal imbalance: when one season extracts far more heat than the other injects, the ground temperature drifts over years. A non-dominant peaker rebalances extraction/rejection, protecting long-term efficiency. Smaller borefield savings but better ground stability.

Both — adds a peaker on each side. Recommended when heating and cooling loads are nearly equal, or in climates with both harsh winters and hot summers.

None — no supplemental system. Sizes borefield for the full peak load. Highest drilling cost but 100% ground-source coverage.
